# My Favorite Series: That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

I really enjoy **That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime** (also known as *Tensura*). It is an incredibly engaging fantasy isekai series with excellent world-building and character development.

## Plot Overview
The story follows **Satoru Mikami**, a 37-year-old corporate worker who gets stabbed and reincarnates in a fantasy world as a blind, gelatinous slime named **Rimuru Tempest**. Instead of being weak, he gains unique skills like *Predator*, allowing him to absorb anything and mimic its abilities.

## Key Highlights
- **World-Building:** Watching Rimuru build the Jura Tempest Federation from a small goblin village into a powerful nation of monsters.
- **Power System:** The evolution of skills from common abilities to Ultimate Skills.
- **Characters:** Memorable companions like Benimaru, Shion, and Diablo.

> "I'm not a bad slime, you know!" 
> — *Rimuru Tempest*
